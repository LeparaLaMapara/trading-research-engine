### `2026-W40-1` From Forecast to Position: Order-Book Signals, Market Impact, and Causal Trading

applied and numerical mathematics, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.22759533](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22759533), read: abstract. **priority 8.0**

* **Concept:** Decision theory under uncertainty and transaction costs in quantitative finance.
* **Market analogue:** The 'order-book forecast' can be any directional market signal the engine generates (e.g., a trend signal). 'Purely proportional costs' are analogous to the measured bid-ask spread on the engine's CFD instruments. 'Forecast uncertainty' can be approximated by the historical volatility of the asset or the forecast error. The 'no-trade region' is a range around the zero-point of the forecast where no new position is initiated because the expected gain does not sufficiently compensate for costs and uncertainty.
* **Claim:** A trading decision rule can be derived that incorporates a 'no-trade region' based on transaction costs and forecast uncertainty, where these two factors widen the same threshold for initiating a trade.
* **Hypothesis:** The profitability of the engine's previously tested ETHUSD 20-day trend rule (E07), which failed audit A01 due to recent underperformance, can be improved by introducing a 'no-trade region'. This region will prevent trade initiation unless the 20-day trend signal (e.g., `close - 20-day MA`) exceeds a dynamic threshold that is widened by both the measured transaction costs (spread) and the historical volatility of ETHUSD returns, thereby filtering out low-conviction trades.
* **Test:** 1. Replicate the ETHUSD 20-day trend rule (E07) on historical data: The original rule buys if `close > 20-day MA` and sells if `close < 20-day MA`. When a position is open, it is held until the trend reverses. 2. Calculate the daily trend signal strength: `Signal = close - 20-day MA` (in price units). 3. Define a dynamic threshold `T` in absolute price terms as: `T = (2.0 * Measured_ETHUSD_Spread) + (1.0 * Rolling_20_Day_StDev_Daily_Log_Returns * ETHUSD_Close_Price)`. The `Measured_ETHUSD_Spread` will be the median spread observed for ETHUSD at the 21:00 UTC D1 bar close time, representing the cost of execution. `Rolling_20_Day_StDev_Daily_Log_Returns` is the standard deviation of the past 20 daily log returns of ETHUSD. The constant `2.0` for spread accounts for estimated round-trip transaction costs, and `1.0` for volatility uses one standard deviation as the uncertainty buffer. 4. Modify the E07 rule for *new trade initiation*: Initiate a long trade only if `Signal > T` and no position is currently open. Initiate a short trade only if `Signal < -T` and no position is currently open. If a long position is open, close it if `Signal < 0`. If a short position is open, close it if `Signal > 0`. If no position is open and the signal is within `[-T, T]`, remain flat. 5. Compare the historical performance (Sharpe ratio, net profit, drawdown, and passing audit criteria: beat random-sign null at 99th percentile, survive doubled costs, most years positive) of this modified rule against the original E07 rule (as it performed in audit A01 and subsequently). The null hypothesis is that the modified rule will not achieve a statistically significant improvement in Sharpe ratio compared to the original E07 rule, or will not pass the engine's audit criteria.
> purely proportional costs create threshold decisions with different error rates.
> It gives an explicit portfolio no-trade region and a scalar rule in which transaction costs and forecast uncertainty widen the same threshold.

### `2026-W40-2` Multivariate quadratic Hawkes processes—part II: non-parametric empirical calibration

physics, [doi:10.1080/14697688.2026.2705206](https://doi.org/10.1080/14697688.2026.2705206), read: truncated at 120000 chars of 122588. **KILLED**

* **Concept:** Leverage effect, a concept from financial economics where negative returns are associated with increased future volatility, is here extended to a cross-asset context and framed within a self-exciting point process (Hawkes process) model. * **Market analogue:** The 'e-mini futures contract' serves as a highly liquid, broad market index instrument in the paper; its analogue for the engine's instruments is ETHUSD, given its dominance in the cryptocurrency space. 'Residual volatility of single stocks' refers to the idiosyncratic volatility of individual stocks after accounting for the market factor; its analogue for the engine could be the residual volatility of other altcoin CFDs (ADAUSD, BCHUSD, LTCUSD) relative to ETHUSD. The 'sign of the return' corresponds to the direction of the daily log-return, and 'volatility' corresponds to future daily realized residual volatility.
* **Claim:** A cross-leverage effect, whereby the sign of the return of one asset impacts the volatility of another asset, is clearly identified, with negative returns of a market factor increasing the residual volatility of individual assets.
* **Hypothesis:** For the engine's available crypto CFDs, a negative daily log-return of ETHUSD will predict higher future daily realized residual volatility for ADAUSD, BCHUSD, and LTCUSD CFDs.
* **Test:** 1. For each target crypto CFD (ADAUSD, BCHUSD, LTCUSD), calculate daily log returns (r_i,t) and for ETHUSD (r_ETHUSD,t). 2. Over the entire historical period (from 2014 or earliest available date), regress r_i,t on r_ETHUSD,t to obtain the estimated static beta (β_i) and the time series of residual returns (e_i,t = r_i,t - β_i * r_ETHUSD,t). 3. Calculate the daily realized residual volatility (σ^2_e_i,t = e^2_i,t). 4. For each target crypto i, regress σ^2_e_i,t on r_ETHUSD,t-1: σ^2_e_i,t = γ_0 + γ_1 * r_ETHUSD,t-1 + ζ_t. 5. The null hypothesis is γ_1 = 0. Failure would be γ_1 >= 0 or a p-value > 0.01 for γ_1 after accounting for multiple testing. This test runs on historical data.
* **Red team, fatal:** The engine's binding constraint is the cost of trading, and this hypothesis, even if true, does not offer a clear path to a profitable trading strategy given the available instruments and cost structure.

### `2026-W40-7` Weight Determination and Portfolio Construction Based on Interval and Distributional Surface Forecasts in Financial Engineering

* **Hypothesis:** A trend-following strategy on ETHUSD that adjusts its position size based on the statistical uncertainty of its trend signal (where the effective expected return `^μ_effective = ^μ - c * κ` for long positions and `^μ_effective = ^μ + c * κ` for short positions, with `κ` being the standard error of the trend estimate and `c` a calibration constant) will achieve a higher Sharpe ratio after trading costs than the engine's current fixed-size trend-following strategy (E07), particularly in the post-September 2022 period.

Otherwise, target a position size proportional to `|mu_effective|`, capped by the engine's 6% risk limit and minimum 0.01 lots.
