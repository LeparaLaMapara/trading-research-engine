### `2026-W40-1` A reproducible protocol for auditing XAI-based trading policies: A case study on the S&P/BVL Peru General Index

statistics, [doi:10.1016/j.array.2026.101257](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.array.2026.101257), read: abstract. **priority 12.5**

* **Concept:** Statistical auditing and rigorous hypothesis testing methodologies, particularly from the field of Explainable AI (XAI) for trading policies.
* **Market analogue:** The audit protocol provides a template for critically evaluating the performance claims of any automated trading strategy, not just XAI-based ones, to prevent overfitting, data snooping, and false positives before or during live deployment. The 'explainability-ablated baseline' can be generalized to a 'feature-ablated baseline' or 'complexity-ablated baseline' for non-XAI models, and 'temporal permutation control' offers a robust null hypothesis generation method.
* **Claim:** A reproducible audit protocol for trading policies, incorporating rigorous controls like chronological separation, ablation, and multiplicity control, can reveal that previously reported favorable findings do not survive stricter testing.
* **Hypothesis:** The current live strategies, E07 (Ethereum 20-day trend) and E08 (USDSGD 20-day trend), when subjected to a rigorous audit protocol including "next-label embargoes", "validation-only hyperparameter selection", "temporal permutation control", and "bootstrap/multiplicity-corrected uncertainty", will show that their reported edge does not survive these stricter tests, leading to "adjusted tests [that] are null" or "paired intervals [that] reach zero".
* **Test:** Apply a rigorous audit protocol, incorporating chronological separation, next-label embargoes, and validation-only hyperparameter selection, to a historical re-evaluation of strategies E07 (Ethereum 20-day trend) and E08 (USDSGD 20-day trend). The null hypothesis for each strategy is that its net PnL (after all trading costs, including spread and financing) is not statistically different from the net PnL of the same strategy applied to a time-permuted version of its input price series (serving as a 'temporal permutation control'). Use bootstrap and multiplicity-corrected uncertainty to construct confidence intervals for the strategy's performance relative to this permuted null. The test passes if the observed net PnL of E07 and E08, when compared to their respective permuted null distributions, demonstrates a statistically significant edge (e.g., the lower bound of the confidence interval for the difference is above zero). Failure would be if 'adjusted tests are null' or 'paired intervals reach zero', indicating a lack of robust statistical significance for the previously reported edge. This re-evaluation will run on historical data, not consuming a live slot.
> Explainability methods increasingly inform real-time trading interventions, but claims of “behavioral correction” in this literature are rarely tested under the chronological separation, ablation, and multiplicity control that such claims require.
> We introduce a reproducible audit protocol — explicit train/validation/test roles with next-label embargoes, validation-only hyperparameter selection, close- t to open- t + 1 execution, an explainability-ablated baseline, a temporal permutation control, and bootstrap/multiplicity-corrected uncertainty — and apply it as a stress test to a previously reported CXAI-Guided Dynamic Stop-Loss (CGDSL) case study (3244 daily S&P/BVL Peru General Index observations, 2011–2024).
