## 3. How it works (the pipeline)

```
bars (market/bars.py, data/market.db)
  -> council proposes (Claude opus-5 proposes, Gemini 2.5 flash red teams; council/)
  -> validator rejects martingale/grid (strategy/validator.py)
  -> failure registry: exact variant tabu, expiring (research/registry.py)
  -> cost ceiling: delete anything costing > 0.13 Sharpe/yr BEFORE testing (research/cost_ceiling.py)
  -> walk forward refit backtest with measured spreads (backtest/walkforward.py)
  -> deflated Sharpe vs honest trial count N, bar 0.95 (same file)
  -> calibration of the LLM's predictions, lessons, leaderboard (research/)
  -> Supabase sink + dashboard (research/supabase_sink.py, dashboard/app.py)
```

Key modules: `strategy/` (buy_hold, random, ma_trend, vol_scaled, multi_timeframe,
mean_reversion, ou_reversion, kalman_level, plus `buffer.py` no trade buffers);
`research/diversify.py` (breadth / IDM maths); `research/goals.py` (family goal layer,
deterministic feasibility); `scout/` (arXiv papers to strategy cards); `ops/audit.py`
(self audit, UNKNOWN counts as FAIL); `scripts/` (sweep_universe, deflate_sweep,
fetch_universe, probe, reconcile, rehearsal, scheduler, preflight).

## 6. What has been found (honest state)

* H1 was a cost trap: 1 of 27 pairs positive on dev and holdout where chance predicts about 7.
  Same rule is 20 to 30 times cheaper at D1.

  chance (0.87x). No evidence of edge.
* Deflated against N = 210 candidates: **19 of 19 fail**. Best looking was XAUEUR vol_scaled,
  holdout Sharpe 1.80, still DSR 0.795 because 2.4 years of holdout is too short to tell 1.8
  from luck.
* Registry now holds 62 `failed_deflated_sharpe` + 4 `too_expensive`. LLM calibration: 63
  predictions, field accuracy 0.43 (worse than a coin).
* **Nothing has ever passed the bar. Nothing should be traded yet.**

## 9. What the owner asked for today, and the plan that was about to start

Owner request (2026-09-19): "get it done today, get the data, train as many models as you

The evidence says the lever is **breadth plus pooling**, not a cleverer single instrument
rule. Carver: one instrument Sharpe 0.35, eight near independent streams 0.61. Per
instrument fitting on ~3,000 daily bars is also what makes ML useless here; pooling across
instruments multiplies the sample.

Planned steps:

1. **Data:** fetch D1 (12y) for ~25 to 35 more liquid live symbols from `data/live_symbols.json`
   via `market.bars` / `scripts/fetch_universe.py` (add them to `RESEARCH_UNIVERSE`). Serial
   only: ONE broker session per account at a time, a second one fails silently.
2. **Panel model zoo** (new package, e.g. `models/` + `scripts/train_zoo.py`): features per
   instrument per day (multi horizon vol normalised returns, EWMA crossovers, breakout
   position, vol regime, cross asset context such as dollar and US500); target next day and
   next 5 day vol normalised return. Models: Carver EWMAC trend ensemble, breakout,
   cross sectional momentum within asset class, pooled ridge and logistic, XGBoost (GPU),
   random forest / extra trees, MLP and small LSTM/TCN in torch (RTX 2070 Super, 8GB), and
   an equal weight forecast ensemble.
3. **Honest evaluation:** expanding window walk forward by year with a purge/embargo; last ~2
   years held out and touched once; forecasts to positions with per instrument vol
   targeting, no trade buffers, measured costs; score at the PORTFOLIO level; deflate every

4. **Champion:** pick on dev, confirm once on holdout, deflate. If it passes, wire daily

## 11. Traps that already cost time

* cTrader spot and trendbar prices are scaled by a fixed 10^5, not 10^digits.
* Order rejections arrive as `ProtoOAOrderErrorEvent` (payloadType 2132); handle it or the
  call silently times out.
* One authenticated session per account: never run the logger and a fetch together.
* Stored Sharpes are annualised; deflation works per observation. Divide by sqrt(bars per
  year) before mixing them.
* A model whose fitted lookback exceeds the test window scores 0.0: that is NOT evaluable,
  not "failed". Keep the distinction.
* Every candidate scored must be logged, or the multiple testing N is a lie.
* `google-generativeai` breaks the cTrader protobuf pin; Gemini is called over REST.
* Windows: `git worktree remove` can follow a node_modules junction and delete the real one.
