**Reading.**
1. CFD financing is 1.5x to 3.2x the whole gross edge for every rule. Confirms run 1 for all rules.
2. Cost gate (charter rule 5, gross >= 3x cost, i.e. spread/gross <= 0.33): tsmom_12m passes
   (0.21); ewmac and blend are on the line (0.32); breakout fails (0.79): EDGE_DESTROYED_BY_COSTS.
3. Even the best, tsmom_12m at 0.25 over 20 years, has a t-stat near 1.1 (standard error about
   1/sqrt(20) = 0.22). Not distinguishable from luck on its own; E19 must be judged on its
   pre-registered test, not on this.
4. The edge is gone since 2017 in every rule except in metals and bonds. Hypothesis: the post-2017
   decay matches the published record of trend funds; it is a reason to expect Sharpe well under 0.3.
5. The class split is descriptive only. Choosing classes from it would be selection on the
   result; E19's universe must be fixed by what micro futures exist, before testing.
