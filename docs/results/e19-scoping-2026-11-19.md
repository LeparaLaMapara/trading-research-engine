# E19 scoping: why trend "failed" here (2026-11-19)

Re-reading the 2026-09-19 long-history test (39 Dukascopy daily markets, 2004 to the holdout
start; holdout untouched). Script: `lab/e19/cost_split.py`. Counted as 2 trials in E19.

| rule | gross Sharpe | net, CFD costs (spread + financing) | net, spread only (futures-like) |
|---|---|---|---|
| tsmom_12m (sign of 12-month return) | 0.31 | -0.23 | **+0.25** |

Net by period, spread only: 2004-08 0.41, 2009-16 0.33, 2017-24 0.01.

Median CFD financing charged, per year of notional: energy 13.5%, indices 5.9%, metals 1.7%,
FX 0.9-1.2%. Spread costs are small next to that.

**Finding.** CFD financing was the largest measured drag on slow trend at this broker, larger
than the whole gross edge. The 2026-09-20 label NO_PREDICTIVE_EDGE does not fit slow trend
(gross was positive), but the signal itself is NOT established: gross 0.31 is about t 1.4 and
net 0.25 about t 1.1 over 20 years, on spot proxies, one of four rules looked at.

**Consequences.**
1. No multi-week CFD holds on cTrader, any strategy. Slow strategies go to futures (IBKR) or
   unlevered ETFs (EasyEquities).
2. E19 is re-scoped to a futures cost model (spread + commission + roll, no CFD financing).
3. The edge has weakened since 2017, as in the published record (SG Trend index flat 2011-19).
   Expect a modest Sharpe; the value of trend is diversification and crisis returns.

Caveats: spread-only on a price index ignores dividends minus the risk-free rate on equity
futures and the interest differential on FX forwards (roughly offsetting over 2004-24, not
exact). The ewmac rerun returned zeros (scaling bug in this scoping script), to fix before E19.
